The font program is embedded into the Tagged PDF/A-2b and PDF/UA-1 export
(core/vocument-layout/src/pdf_export/font_embedding.rs) as an authentic multi-script
OpenType/Unicode fallback font covering CJK, Greek, Cyrillic, and Mathematical symbols.
