## 3. Manual Verification Checklist for Unavailable Foreign Viewers

For QA teams executing manual acceptance testing on environments with Adobe Acrobat Reader, PDF-XChange, or Mozilla Firefox installed:

1. **Target Artifact:** Use the PDF generated by `document_core_run_interop_gate_suite()` or `core/vocument-core/tests/interop_tests.rs` (`m0_workflow_interop.pdf`).
2. **Page Rotation Check:**
   - Open the PDF in the target viewer.
   - Verify Page 1 displays rotated by 180° (upside down relative to original 0° orientation).
3. **Highlight Annotation Check:**
   - Verify the yellow highlight annotation on Page 1 is visible across the text rect `[72, 700, 300, 720] pt`.
   - Click/tap or hover the highlight annotation to verify author (`"Vocument Interop"`) and note contents (`"Verified highlight"`).
4. **Console / Structural Integrity Check:**
   - In Acrobat: Check *File -> Properties* and verify PDF version (`1.7` / `1.4`) and no repair or corruption warnings on open.
   - In Firefox: Open Web Console (`Cmd+Option+K` / `F12`) and verify PDF.js outputs zero syntax or xref repair errors.
   - In PDF-XChange: Verify document opens cleanly without repair prompt.

### 4.1 Platform scope of the interoperability gate

The interoperability gate requires the document to reopen in a fresh MuPDF session
**and** to open in at least one foreign reference renderer:

- `mutool` is the portable reference renderer and is used on every supported
  development host.
- `sips` (macOS CoreGraphics/Quartz) and Chrome PDFium are macOS-specific
  additions. They are additionally **required** on macOS hosts, where this
  acceptance record was produced, and are reported honestly as unavailable
  elsewhere instead of being simulated.
